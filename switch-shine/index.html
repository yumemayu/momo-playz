Switch & Shine

How to play

  1. Tap a round button on the left to switch between 0 and 1.
  2. Watch the lights on the right. 1 means on and 0 means off. In Lab, tap › to try the next circuit.
  3. In Challenge, match every light on the right to the goal above the board.
